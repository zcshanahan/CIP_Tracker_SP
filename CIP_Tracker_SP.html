Welcome to the CIP Opportunity Tracker

This is your BD command center for Colorado water, sewer, and environmental projects — 328 live opportunities in one place.

Takes about 90 seconds to get oriented. Let's go.

👤

First: tell it who you are

See the "I am…" dropdown in the top-right corner? Pick your name.

Once you pick your name, you can:
• Claim projects with the Owner dropdown on each row
• Hit "My Projects" to see only yours
• Sign outreach emails automatically with your name and contact info

Your selections save in your browser — you won't have to do this again.

🎯

Find what matters to you

There are 328 projects — here's how to cut through them fast:

  • 1
    Filter Priority → 🔴 Hot to see RFPs that are imminent or dropping soon
  • 2
    Filter Agency to focus on a specific client you already know
  • 3
    Check 🟣 Junior-friendly for smaller projects good for a younger engineer to own the relationship
  • 4
    Use Owner → Unassigned to see what's unclaimed and needs a home
✉️

Reach out in 30 seconds

Every project with a known contact has a ✉ Reach Out button.

  • 1
    Click ✉ Reach Out on any row
  • 2
    A pre-written email draft opens — tweak the first line to make it yours
  • 3
    Hit 📋 Copy to clipboard and paste into Outlook or Gmail
💡 The goal of the email is a 15-minute call — not a pitch. Keep it short and curious.
🚀

You're ready. One last thing.

Use the Notes field on each row to jot next actions, dates, or what was said on a call. Notes save automatically in your browser.

Suggested routine: Once a week, filter to 🔴 Hot + your name. Check if anything needs a follow-up. Takes 5 minutes.
